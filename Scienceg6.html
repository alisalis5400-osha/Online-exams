🧪 امتحان Science المقالي الذكي (الصف السادس الابتدائي)

أجب عن الأسئلة الآتية بأسلوبك العلمي، وسيتم تقييم إجابتك وعرض الإجابة النموذجية فوراً.

1. Give reason: Why does clear limewater turn milky white when exhaled air is blown into it?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Because exhaled air contains carbon dioxide.
2. Give reason: Why does clear limewater remain clear when room air is blown into it?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Because room air contains a very small amount of carbon dioxide.
3. Give reason: Why does the chest get bigger when a person inhales?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Because air enters and fills the lungs.
4. Give reason: Why does the heart work alongside the lungs during respiration?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Because the heart pumps oxygen-rich blood to the whole body.
5. Give reason: Why can oxygen taken in by the lungs reach the fingertips and toes?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Because the blood carries oxygen to the whole body.
6. Give reason: Alveoli are surrounded by a network of blood vessels.
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Because gas exchange takes place between the air and the blood.
7. What happens if: You breathe in (inhale) air through your nose or mouth?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Air goes to the lungs, and the chest gets bigger.
8. What happens if: You breathe out (exhale) air?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: The chest gets smaller, and air rich in carbon dioxide leaves the body.
9. What happens if: You blow exhaled air into clear limewater and shake it?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: The limewater turns milky white because of carbon dioxide.
10. What happens if: Blood passes around the alveoli in the lungs?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Gas exchange takes place in the alveoli.
11. What happens if: A fish breathes?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: It takes in oxygen from water through its gills and releases carbon dioxide into the water.
12. What happens if: There is an absence of alveoli in the lungs?
الإجابة النموذجية المثالية: Gas exchange will not take place.
