Mr. Adel Elamin - Science Teacher | Grade 6
A. Complete the Following Sentences
1. Taking in oxygen and letting out carbon dioxide is called...
Model Answer: Respiration
2. Air passes from the nose and mouth through the ... before it reaches the lungs.
Model Answer: windpipe
3. The two organs in the chest that receive air are the left and right ...
Model Answer: lungs
4. The enlarged structure used in the model to observe where gases are exchanged is an ...
Model Answer: alveolus
5. The ... around the lungs provide the route through which gas exchange occurs.
Model Answer: blood vessels
6. The gas taken from the air into the blood is ...
Model Answer: oxygen
7. The gas released from the blood into the air is ...
Model Answer: carbon dioxide
8. The ... carries oxygen from the lungs to the whole body.
Model Answer: blood
9. The ... does the work that helps transport oxygen-rich blood around the body.
Model Answer: heart
10. Fish let out carbon dioxide through their ...
Model Answer: gills

F. Give Reasons
1. Why is breathed-out air different from breathed-in air?
Model Answer: Because body cells use oxygen and produce carbon dioxide.
2. Why does the investigation use a bag filled with room air as well as breathed-out air?
Model Answer: To provide a baseline comparison.
3. Why are limewater and gas detector tubes useful in the investigation?
Model Answer: To detect and measure gas amounts accurately.
4. Why are the blood vessels around the lungs important?
Model Answer: For gas exchange between air and blood.
5. How can oxygen taken in by the lungs reach distant parts of the body?
Model Answer: Carried by blood pumped by the heart.
